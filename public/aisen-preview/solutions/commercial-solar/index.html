WHAT IT IS

Commercial Solar क्या है?

Commercial solar is designed for offices, retail stores, malls, schools, hospitals, hotels, institutions and other business properties. Because commercial loads are often highest during sunny hours, a well-designed system can use a large share of generation directly and deliver strong operating savings.

✓ Reduce operating expenditure✓ Improve long-term tariff visibility✓ Use daytime generation efficiently✓ Strengthen ESG and green-brand credentials

01

System sizing

Capacity is optimized against daytime base load, maximum demand, sanctioned load, roof area and export regulations. The goal is not simply to fill the roof; it is to maximize financially useful generation.

02

Investment & savings

Commercial proposals should be assessed using project cost, expected annual generation, self-consumption, tariff, degradation, O&M and financing. Aisen provides a site-specific savings and payback model.

03

Maintenance & support

Commercial systems need scheduled cleaning, generation review, thermography/inspection where appropriate, torque and protection checks, inverter service coordination and documented preventive maintenance.
