WHAT IT IS

Hybrid Solar & Storage क्या है?

A hybrid solar system integrates PV panels, a hybrid inverter, battery storage and the utility grid. It can prioritize solar for current loads, store excess energy, use the grid when required and supply selected backup loads during an outage—according to the configured energy strategy.

✓ Backup for essential loads✓ Solar energy after sunset✓ Intelligent source management✓ Expandable battery architecture

01

System sizing

Battery sizing begins with the essential-load list, wattage, required backup duration, surge loads and usable depth of discharge. Solar capacity and battery capacity are related but must be calculated separately.

02

Investment & savings

Hybrid systems cost more than on-grid systems because of batteries, control equipment and backup distribution. The right design balances desired backup hours, battery life, future expandability and budget.

03

Maintenance & support

Modern lithium systems are relatively low-maintenance but require ventilation/temperature compliance, firmware and monitoring checks, safe clearances and periodic inspection of connections and protection devices.
