Industrial Solar EPC क्या है?

Industrial solar EPC covers engineering, procurement and construction of medium-to-large PV systems for factories, warehouses, manufacturing plants and industrial campuses. It requires detailed structural, electrical, safety, generation and execution planning around live operations.

01

System sizing

Industrial sizing considers load curves, contract demand, transformer capacity, roof loading, usable zones, shadow, evacuation design and regulations. Detailed engineering may include structural certification and energy-yield simulation.

02

Investment & savings

Project economics use capex, annual yield, tariff, self-consumption, depreciation/tax treatment where applicable, financing, module degradation and O&M. Final commercial assumptions should be reviewed with your finance and tax advisers.

03

Maintenance & support

Industrial O&M includes planned cleaning, SCADA review, preventive electrical checks, thermal inspection, vegetation/roof upkeep, spares planning, safety audits and documented response procedures.
