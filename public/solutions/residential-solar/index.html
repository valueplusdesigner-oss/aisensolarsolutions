WHAT IT IS

Residential Solar क्या है?

Residential solar is a rooftop photovoltaic system designed for homes, villas, apartments and housing societies. Solar panels convert sunlight into DC electricity; an inverter converts it into AC electricity used by your appliances. In an on-grid system, surplus energy can be exported through net metering where applicable.

✓ Lower monthly electricity bills✓ Subsidy guidance for eligible homes✓ 25-year panel performance life✓ Clean, silent and low-maintenance power

SYSTEM OPTIONS

मुख्य प्रकार

On-grid solar

Connected to the utility grid. It offers the best economics and can export eligible surplus generation through net metering.

Off-grid solar

Uses batteries and works independently from the grid. Suitable for remote locations or areas with unreliable supply.

Hybrid solar

Combines grid power, solar and battery backup for savings plus essential-load resilience during outages.

01

System sizing

System size depends on electricity consumption, sanctioned load, usable shadow-free roof and local regulations. As a broad planning guide, 1 kW generally needs about 80–100 sq.ft. of clear roof area.

02

Investment & savings

Cost varies with capacity, panel technology, inverter, mounting structure, roof conditions and safety requirements. A site survey is required for an accurate proposal. Savings depend on tariff, generation and how much solar energy is consumed on-site.

03

Maintenance & support

Keep panels clean, monitor generation and arrange periodic electrical/safety inspection. There are no moving parts, so routine maintenance is limited. Aisen also provides AMC and performance support.
